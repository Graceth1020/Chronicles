### 1、Examples
- accustomed to(习惯于)：He **has become** accustomed to the life here since he **moved** to the city last year.
- amount to(达到)：The total cost **will amount to** more than 10,000 dollars if we **carry** on the project.
- appeal to(呼吁)：The charity **appealed to** the public for help when they **met** a shortage of money.
- apply to(适用于)：This rule **applies to** all students who **study** in this school.
- attached to(附属于)：The research center **is attached to** the university that **was built** in 1950.
- attend to(处理/照料)：The nurse **was attending to** the patient when the doctor **arrived**.
- belong to(属于)：This old book **belonged to** my grandfather before he **passed away**.
- challenge to(向……挑战)：He **challenged** the top player to a match that **attracted** many people.
- compare to/with(比较)：If you **compare** this work with that one, you **will find** big differences.
- condemned to(判刑)：The man **was condemned to** prison because he **had broken** the law.
- confess to(承认)：She **confessed to** the mistake that she **had made** in the work.
- confine to(限制)：The patient **has been confined to** bed since he **fell** ill last week.
- consent to(同意)：My parents **consented to** my plan after I **explained** it carefully.
- convert to(改信/转变)：He **converted to** another religion when he **was** living abroad.
- entitled to(享有)：Everyone **is entitled to** freedom that **is** protected by law.
- listen to(听)：She **was listening to** music when the phone **rang** last night.
- mention to(提到)：He **mentioned to** me the plan that he **had thought** of before.
- object to(反对)：Many people **object to** the new policy that **was** announced yesterday.
- occur to(想到)：A good idea **occurred to** me when I **was walking** home.
- prefer to(更喜欢)：I **prefer to** stay at home rather than go out when it **rains**.
- react to(对……反应)：The child **reacted to** the news quickly because he **understood** it.
- reply to(回复)：He **has replied to** the email that you **sent** last week.
- respond to(响应)：The public **responded to** the activity warmly when it **started**.
- see to(处理/负责)：I **will see to** the matter before you **come** back tomorrow.
- submit to(服从)：He **refused to** submit to the rule that he **thought** unfair.
- surrender to(投降)：The soldiers **surrendered to** the enemy after the war **ended**.
- turn to(求助于)：She **turned to** her friends for help when she **had** problems.
- yield to(屈服)：He **never yields to** difficulties no matter what **happens**.
