  1. The local community relies __ the river for both drinking water and irrigation.

  2. After living abroad for years, she has gradually accustom __ the local cuisine and lifestyle.

  3. The board of directors strongly objected __ the proposed restructuring plan.

  7. The young entrepreneur embarked __ an ambitious journey to revolutionize the industry.

No. Answer No. Answer

  3. 董事会强烈反对拟议的重组计划。
