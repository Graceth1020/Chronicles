📝 练习题（101个填空）

Section 1（第1-10题）

  1. After months of training, she is finally qualified __ the national championship.

  2. The old man was strangely familiar __ the village despite never having visited it before.

  3. You should not be jealous __ your sister’s success; work harder instead.

  4. This brand is famous __ producing high-quality leather goods.

  5. The witness was reluctant __ provide more details because he felt threatened.

  6. Fresh vegetables are sufficient __ meeting our daily nutritional needs.

  7. He is rather weak __ mathematics, but his writing skills are exceptional.

  8. I am deeply grateful __ my mentor for guiding me through graduate school.

  9. The new software is fully consistent __ the existing operating system.

  10. She remained ignorant __ the serious consequences of her actions until it was too late.

✅ Section 1 答案（完整介词短语）
No. Answer (完整短语) No. Answer (完整短语)
1 qualified for 6 sufficient for
2 familiar with/to 7 weak in
3 jealous of 8 grateful to
4 famous for 9 consistent with
5 reluctant about/to 10 ignorant of
🌏 Section 1 翻译
  1. 经过几个月的训练，她终于有资格参加全国锦标赛了。
  2. 那位老人对这个村庄有一种奇怪的熟悉感，尽管他从未去过。
  3. 你不应该嫉妒你妹妹的成功；你应该更加努力。
  4. 这个品牌以生产高品质皮具而闻名。
  5. 证人因为感到受威胁而不愿意提供更多细节。
  6. 新鲜蔬菜足以满足我们每日的营养需求。
  7. 他数学相当薄弱，但写作能力很出色。
  8. 我深深感激我的导师在研究生期间对我的指导。
  9. 这款新软件与现有操作系统完全兼容。
  10. 直到为时已晚，她仍然对自己行为的严重后果一无所知。

Section 2（第11-20题）

  1. The professor is very popular __ his students because of his humorous teaching style.

  2. We need to find a solution that is acceptable __ all parties involved.

  3. The little boy was curious __ how airplanes can stay in the sky for so long.

  4. You are responsible __ ensuring that all tasks are completed by Friday.

  5. The two samples are identical __ each other under microscopic examination.

  6. He is intent __ finishing his novel before the end of the year.

  7. My grandmother is not very efficient __ using modern smartphones.

  8. The director was angry __ the actor for forgetting his lines repeatedly.

  9. This historic building is situated south __ the main square.

  10. She felt uneasy __ walking alone in the deserted neighborhood at night.

✅ Section 2 答案（完整介词短语）
No. Answer (完整短语) No. Answer (完整短语)
11 popular with 16 intent on
12 acceptable to 17 efficient at/in
13 curious about 18 angry with
14 responsible for 19 south of
15 identical to 20 uneasy about
🌏 Section 2 翻译
  1. 这位教授因其幽默的教学风格在学生中很受欢迎。
  2. 我们需要找到一个所有相关方都能接受的解决方案。
  3. 小男孩对飞机如何能在空中停留这么久感到好奇。
  4. 你负责确保所有任务在周五前完成。
  5. 在显微镜检查下，这两个样本完全相同。
  6. 他专心致志要在年底前完成他的小说。
  7. 我祖母不太会使用现代智能手机。
  8. 导演因为演员反复忘记台词而生气。
  9. 这座历史建筑位于主广场以南。
  10. 她对深夜独自走在荒芜的街区感到不安。

Section 3（第21-30题）

  1. His political views are contrary __ everything our party stands for.

  2. The manager is busy __ preparing the annual report all week.

  3. You should be careful __ your belongings when traveling through crowded stations.

  4. The young athlete is keen __ pursuing a professional career in basketball.

  5. The scientific community remains doubtful __ the authenticity of those experimental results.

  6. This passport is valid __ five years from the date of issue.

  7. Be patient __ the elderly; they need more time to express themselves.

  8. The company is independent __ any government funding or support.

  9. She was sad __ hearing that her childhood friend had moved away permanently.

  10. The children were intent __ winning the competition, practicing day and night.

✅ Section 3 答案（完整介词短语）
No. Answer (完整短语) No. Answer (完整短语)
21 contrary to 26 valid for
22 busy with/at 27 patient with
23 careful of/with 28 independent of
24 keen on 29 sad about/at
25 doubtful about/of 30 intent on
🌏 Section 3 翻译
  1. 他的政治观点与我们党的主张完全相反。
  2. 经理这整周都忙于准备年度报告。
  3. 在拥挤的车站旅行时，你应该小心保管好自己的物品。
  4. 这位年轻的运动员热衷于追求职业篮球生涯。
  5. 科学界对这些实验结果的真实性仍持怀疑态度。
  6. 这本护照自签发之日起有效期为五年。
  7. 对老人要有耐心；他们需要更多时间来表达自己。
  8. 这家公司不依赖任何政府资助或支持，保持独立。
  9. 听到她的童年好友永久搬走的消息，她很难过。
  10. 孩子们一心想要赢得比赛，日以继夜地练习。

Section 4（第31-40题）

  1. I am not entirely convinced __ his explanation for being late this morning.

  2. This knife is primarily used __ cutting bread and soft fruits.

  3. The team was fortunate __ having an experienced leader during the crisis.

  4. He is completely unaware __ the danger that lies ahead.

  5. Your actions are not consistent __ the values you claim to hold.

  6. The tourists were curious __ the ancient ruins they had read about in guidebooks.

  7. She is capable __ handling much more complex projects than this one.

  8. The manager was polite __ everyone, regardless of their position in the company.

  9. After the long journey, we were short __ drinking water and had to ration it.

  10. He is guilty __ violating several traffic laws, according to the police report.

✅ Section 4 答案（完整介词短语）
No. Answer (完整短语) No. Answer (完整短语)

32 used for 37 capable of
33 fortunate in 38 polite to
34 unaware of 39 short of

🌏 Section 4 翻译
  1. 我并不完全相信他今天早上迟到的解释。
  2. 这把刀主要用于切面包和软水果。
  3. 团队很幸运在危机期间有一位经验丰富的领导者。
  4. 他完全没有意识到前方的危险。
  5. 你的行为与你所声称的价值观不一致。
  6. 游客们对他们从旅游指南上读到的古老遗迹感到好奇。
  7. 她有能力处理比这个复杂得多的项目。
  8. 经理对每个人都很礼貌，无论他们在公司职位高低。
  9. 长途旅行后，我们缺乏饮用水，不得不定量分配。
  10. 根据警方报告，他违反了多条交通法规。

Section 5（第41-50题）

  1. The new intern is still new __ the industry and needs thorough training.

  2. This area is famous __ its breathtaking sunsets and scenic hiking trails.

  3. You are right __ believing that hard work pays off in the long run.

  4. She is conscious __ her own weaknesses and works hard to overcome them.

  5. The elderly woman is dependent __ her son for daily care and mobility.

  6. I am sorry __ causing any misunderstanding during yesterday’s meeting.

  7. The company is short __ experienced engineers, which slows down production.

  8. He is fond __ playing classical piano in his spare time.

  9. The solution is obvious __ anyone who has studied basic chemistry.

  10. The two political parties are similar __ each other in many economic aspects.

🌏 Section 5 翻译
  1. 新来的实习生还是这个行业的新手，需要全面培训。
  2. 这个地区以其令人惊叹的日落和风景优美的徒步小径而闻名。
  3. 你相信努力终有回报是正确的。
  4. 她意识到自己的弱点，并努力克服它们。
  5. 这位老妇人依赖儿子提供日常照护和出行帮助。
  6. 我很抱歉在昨天的会议上造成了任何误解。
  7. 公司缺乏经验丰富的工程师，这拖慢了生产进度。
  8. 他喜欢在空闲时间弹古典钢琴。
  9. 解决方案对任何学过基础化学的人来说是显而易见的。
  10. 这两个政党在许多经济方面彼此相似。

Section 6（第51-60题）

  1. She is extremely sensitive __ criticism, so please be gentle with your feedback.

  2. The teacher was patient __ the struggling student, explaining the concept multiple times.

  3. This medicine is harmful __ your liver if taken in excessive amounts.

  4. The president is accountable __ the people for his decisions and policies.

  5. He is certain __ winning the election, according to the latest polls.

  6. The witness was doubtful __ the suspect’s alibi from the very beginning.

  7. You are qualified __ this position only if you have relevant work experience.

  8. The manager is enthusiastic __ implementing new technologies in the workplace.

  9. This decision could prove fatal __ the future of the entire organization.

  10. He is envious __ his neighbor’s luxurious lifestyle and expensive car.

✅ Section 6 答案（完整介词短语）

🌏 Section 6 翻译
  1. 她对批评极其敏感，所以请温和地提出反馈。
  2. 老师对那个学习困难的学生很有耐心，多次解释那个概念。
  3. 过量服用此药会对你的肝脏有害。
  4. 总统要为自己的决策和政策对人民负责。
  5. 根据最新民调，他确信自己会赢得选举。
  6. 目击者从一开始就怀疑嫌疑人的不在场证明。
  7. 只有拥有相关工作经验，你才有资格担任这个职位。
  8. 经理热衷于在工作场所推行新技术。
  9. 这个决定可能对整个组织的未来是致命的。
  10. 他羡慕邻居奢华的生活方式和昂贵的汽车。

Section 7（第61-70题）

  1. The old house is situated north __ the river, hidden among the trees.

  2. The student was quick __ understanding the complex mathematical concept.

  3. You should be aware __ the potential risks before investing your money.

  4. She is faithful __ her principles regardless of the circumstances.

  5. The two theories are different __ each other in fundamental ways.

  6. He is honest __ his business dealings, which earned him a solid reputation.

  7. The child is afraid __ the dark and always sleeps with a nightlight.

  8. I am grateful __ having such supportive colleagues during challenging times.

  9. The hotel is far __ the city center, so renting a car is necessary.

  10. You are rude __ interrupt others while they are speaking, you know.

✅ Section 7 答案（完整介词短语）
No. Answer (完整短语) No. Answer (完整短语)

🌏 Section 7 翻译
  1. 老房子位于河北岸，隐藏在树林之中。
  2. 那个学生很快理解了这个复杂的数学概念。
  3. 在投资之前，你应该意识到潜在的风险。
  4. 无论环境如何，她都忠于自己的原则。
  5. 这两种理论在根本上是不同的。
  6. 他做生意诚实，这为他赢得了良好的声誉。
  7. 孩子害怕黑暗，总是开着夜灯睡觉。
  8. 我很感激在困难时期有如此支持我的同事。
  9. 酒店远离市中心，所以租车是必要的。
  10. 别人说话时打断别人是很粗鲁的，你知道吗？

Section 8（第71-80题）

  1. The athlete is capable __ breaking the world record if conditions are favorable.

  2. She is indifferent __ criticism; she simply continues doing her work.

  3. The recipe is different __ the traditional version, using fewer spices.

  4. He is dear __ me, and I would do anything to help him.

  5. The professor is expert __ analyzing ancient manuscripts and historical texts.

  6. The child was quick __ learning new languages at a very young age.

  7. I am certain __ having locked the door before leaving the apartment.

  8. The manager is responsible __ training all new employees in the department.

  9. The witness was reluctant __ cooperating with the investigation due to fear.

  10. This path is safe __ wild animals, so you can hike without worry.

✅ Section 8 答案（完整介词短语）

🌏 Section 8 翻译
  1. 如果条件有利，这位运动员有能力打破世界纪录。
  2. 她对批评无动于衷；只是继续做她的工作。
  3. 这个食谱与传统版本不同，用了更少的香料。
  4. 他对我来说很重要，我愿意做任何事帮助他。
  5. 这位教授擅长分析古代手稿和历史文献。
  6. 那个孩子很小的时候就很快学会了新语言。
  7. 我确信离开公寓前锁了门。
  8. 经理负责培训部门内所有新员工。
  9. 由于害怕，证人不愿意配合调查。
  10. 这条小路没有野生动物出没，所以你可以放心徒步。

  1. The team is deficient __ experienced players, which explains their poor performance.

  2. She is skillful __ negotiating complex business deals under pressure.

  3. The young man is keen __ photography and spends hours taking pictures.

  4. The information is identical __ what we received from the other source.

  5. He is guilty __ making false promises during the election campaign.

  6. The professor is conscious __ the difficulties his students are facing.

  7. This region is rich in resources but deficient __ fresh water.

  8. She is jealous __ her colleague’s rapid promotion and higher salary.

  9. The plan is feasible only if we are lucky __ getting government approval.

  10. The new employee is slow __ adapting to the company’s fast-paced environment.

🌏 Section 9 翻译
  1. 球队缺乏经验丰富的球员，这解释了他们的糟糕表现。
  2. 她擅长在压力下谈判复杂的商业交易。
  3. 这位年轻人热爱摄影，花很多时间拍照。
  4. 这些信息与我们从另一个来源收到的完全相同。
  5. 他犯有在竞选期间做出虚假承诺的罪行。
  6. 教授意识到他的学生们正在面临的困难。
  7. 这个地区资源丰富，但缺乏淡水。
  8. 她嫉妒同事的快速晋升和更高的薪水。
  9. 只有我们幸运地获得政府批准，这个计划才可行。
  10. 新员工适应公司快节奏环境的速度很慢。

Section 10（第91-101题）

  1. The teacher is kind __ all students, regardless of their academic performance.

  2. He is confident __ his ability to lead the team through this difficult period.

  3. The driver was guilty __ running the red light, according to the traffic camera.

  4. She is shy __ expressing her true feelings in front of large audiences.

  5. The manager is content __ the current progress and sees no need for changes.

  6. He is weak __ remembering names, which sometimes causes awkward situations.

  7. The company remains independent __ external investors and makes its own decisions.

  8. She is worried __ her son’s health after he caught a severe cold.

  9. The scientist is doubtful __ the validity of the newly published research.

  10. This decision is crucial __ the success of the entire project.

  11. The students are eager __ starting their summer vacation after exhausting exams.

🌏 Section 10 翻译

  2. 他对自己带领团队度过这个困难时期的能力充满信心。
  3. 根据交通摄像头显示，司机犯有闯红灯的罪行。
  4. 她羞于在众多观众面前表达自己的真实感受。
  5. 经理对目前的进展感到满意，认为不需要改变。
  6. 他不擅长记名字，这有时会造成尴尬的情况。
  7. 公司保持独立于外部投资者，自己做决定。
  8. 儿子重感冒后，她担心他的健康。
  9. 科学家对这项新发表的研究的有效性表示怀疑。
  10. 这个决定对整个项目的成功至关重要。
  11. 经过疲惫的考试，学生们渴望开始他们的暑假。
